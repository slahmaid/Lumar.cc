Get into a Turkish university without guessing which form goes where

Started because the ikamet office doesn't take walk-ins on Fridays

LUMAR started with one Moroccan student at Bahçeşehir University who finished with distinction. Then came the mess: wrong document scans, a bank that wanted a student number before enrollment was final, a landlord asking for a Turkish contract nobody in the family could read.

A few of us, students already in Istanbul and alumni back in Morocco, started answering WhatsApp messages from cousins. That's still most of what we do. We don't take every major. If your grades won't pass Medipol's medicine cut-off, we'll tell you before you pay anyone anything.

Typical reply time

Same day on WhatsApp (weekdays)

Application filed

Within 3 working days once docs are complete

What we won't do

Promise a visa outcome we can't control. We handle the university side and the paperwork after acceptance.

Based in

Morocco and Istanbul. We meet students at Sabiha Gökçen when they land.
